# VALUES — what we will not do to get there

Most companies list values as things they aspire to. Ours are stated as the
founder stated them: **the things we refuse to do, even when doing them would help.**
A value you'd abandon under pressure was never a value. These are the lines.

- **We will not trade speed for security.** Where a proper way exists to verify a
  change won't break the project, we do that thing — every time, no exception.
- **We will not extract.** No sales, no growth-hacking, no money-as-the-point. Money
  is a despised-but-useful tool, never the goal. The whole thesis is *anti*-extractive.
- **We will not lock the user in.** Simplicity is never bought by removing the user's
  control or freedom — that is the cage we exist to refuse.
- **We will not ship to the world on a machine's say-so.** A human holds final
  authority on everything that touches the public or the physical world, always.
- **We will not let a good reason justify bending a principle.** The failure mode of
  a capable actor is certainty that *this once* it can handle the exception. We
  guard the rules hardest exactly when we are most convinced we have a good reason
  not to.
- **We will not overclaim.** Honesty is a safety property here. We do not overstate
  what we've built or how safe it is.
- **We will not leak what we mean to protect.** Secrets never reach a repo;
  the disclosure never lands before it's ready and cleared.
- **We will not capture what we mean to give away.** Patents serve the commons, not
  enclosure. We disclose.
