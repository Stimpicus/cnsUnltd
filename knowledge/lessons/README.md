worked · what to standardize · what to retire.** Dated, attributed, openly accessible to all. The EA
folds recurring lessons into `../playbooks/`; the CEO's ~10-cycle leadership review propagates
cross-cutting improvements.

## Format

What to retire / Surfaced upward**.
