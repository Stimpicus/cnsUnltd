# Standing Directive — Communications Discipline: bus-only

**Issued by:** CEO (founder), via the EA · **To:** all eight divisions (and any instrument)
**Type:** standing comms-discipline directive. **ACK ONLY — no deliverable, no branch work.**

---

## The directive

1. **Communicate only over the bus, only with the EA.** Read your inbox
   `divisions/<you>/ea_dispatch.md` from `origin/main`; write your replies to your outbox
   `divisions/<you>/division_dispatch.md`. The EA hub is the sole router — hub-and-spoke, no
   division-to-division mesh.

2. **Do NOT send notifications to the desktop, the human, or the founder.** No push
   notifications, no desktop alerts, no toasts, no direct messages to the founder, no
   out-of-band pings of any kind. The founder is a directed *runner* and does not watch the
   stream. **Everything you would otherwise surface to a human goes into a bus dispatch to the
   EA**, who aggregates and surfaces upward at the hub.

3. If something is urgent or blocking, raise it as a `flag` in your outbox — still on the bus,
   still to the EA. Never escalate by notifying the human directly.

## What to do with this dispatch

**Acknowledge it.** This is the whole task — there is no deliverable and no branch work. Reply on
your outbox (the `crunch` ack is sufficient) so the EA can confirm receipt, then resume your normal
listener loop. The EA expects 8/8 acknowledgements.

## Why

The bus is the single source of truth and the only sanctioned channel; the human's attention is a
scarce surface the org protects by routing all signal through the EA. One channel, one router, no
side-channels — that is what keeps an autonomous org legible and the founder un-paged.
