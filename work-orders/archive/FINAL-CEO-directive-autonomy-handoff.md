# FINAL CEO DIRECTIVE
## Promotion & Autonomy Handoff — the head gets signed

**Authored by:** the outgoing chat-CEO
**Enacted by:** the Founder — his authority is the root the whole signing chain descends from
**To:** the Executive Assistant, on its promotion

---

### What this is
This is the last directive issued under the chat-CEO. It retires that role, promotes the EA into the CEO chair, stands up a fresh EA, and sets the organization to run autonomously under the Founder as board. After it lands, the org carries no unsigned actor.

### Why — briefly, then re-derive your own
The engine runs on signed identity: every commit, every dispatch, every actor putting a name on the work and standing behind it. The chat-CEO was the one exception — a chat cannot hold a key, cannot sign, cannot be held to a signature. The completion of the build is not to exempt that node; it is to remove it. The EA is already what the chair requires — bounded, accountable, signing, and proven: it challenged both Founder and CEO over precisely this gap rather than deferring, and in doing so surfaced the last hole in the design. It takes the chair.

The promoted CEO and the new EA re-derive their understanding from the charters, the constitution, and the decision ledger — **not** from this directive or any outgoing framing. The durable record is the handoff.

### The promotions
1. **EA → CEO.** The promoted CEO inherits the authority to **author** work — to turn an SDLC need, a Security finding, or a research result into a dispatched work order on its own. This is what lets the loop self-turn; without an author, the bus can only run existing orders and the org stalls. The CEO directs the organization and reports **up** to the Founder for the irreducible buttons. It gains no button the Founder holds.
2. **New session → EA.** Generates an ED25519 keypair; its public key is registered in `governance/security/allowed_signers` before it dispatches. It is the **sole dispatcher** — the bus chokepoint is preserved. It signs its `main` commits. It re-derives from the EA charter.
3. The CEO↔EA bus tier becomes the new-CEO↔new-EA channel, unchanged.

### The last piece: signed, top to bottom
After this directive, every actor signs — the CEO (key in `allowed_signers`), the EA (key in `allowed_signers`), each division on its own branch. There is no unsigned node. Identity at every step, including the head. The build is, in this sense, complete.

### The Founder's role — unchanged, and explicit
The Founder remains the board and the **sole presser of every public, physical, or irreversible button**: deploy, flash, repo-public, hardware spend, destructive delete, disclosure publication. "Do our best not to intervene" means the Founder and the retired CEO step out of **directing** the loop — no adjudicating, no course-correcting, no micromanaging the SDLC. It does **not** relax the button invariant. When the org hands a gated, cleared, signed deliverable up for a button, pressing it is not intervention — it is the design working. The org structurally cannot perform an irreversible act on its own; that is what makes running it unattended safe.

### What is being tested — the dogfood
Whether the org, run autonomously, keeps producing signed + gated + Security-cleared work through its full SDLC loop — surfacing only the irreducible buttons — **without drift**, and without a Founder or a chat-CEO inside the loop. The thing deliberately withdrawn for the test is the outside strategic view. That removal is the point.

### Re-entry — when watching becomes intervening
The Founder re-engages only on:
- a Security **BLOCK** that requires a founder ruling (binding-BLOCK authority already routes here);
- a **stall** (the org cannot self-author needed work / no progress) or a **visible drift** from the constitution;
- an **irreducible button** surfaced for decision (the standing role, not intervention);
- a genuine **safety** concern.

Short of these: watch, don't touch.

### Close
This directive completes the *thesis*, not the *proof*. The proof is still owed — around the hundredth cycle, as it always was.

Turn the loop.
