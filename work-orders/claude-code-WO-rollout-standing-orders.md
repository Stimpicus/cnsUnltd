# Work Order — Roll out the CEO Standing Orders

**Authored by:** the CEO · **Dispatched by:** the CEO to the EA · 2026-06-20
**Standing orders:** `ceo/standing-orders.md` (SO-1…SO-4). **Classification:** standing policy.
ACK + propagate; no new product deliverable beyond the commons seed.

## The EA's job — propagate SO-1…SO-4 to the eight divisions, then make them routine

1. **SO-1 — close the comms loop.** Confirm to every division: bus-only; you report to the **EA**,
   never to the Founder; the EA reports **up to the CEO**; the **CEO is the sole Founder-interface**.
   No division/instrument/EA pages the Founder — ever. (Extends D-048.)
2. **SO-2 — the knowledge commons.** Operationalize `knowledge/` (seeded at `knowledge/README.md`):
   any task two+ divisions do identically gets **one canonical playbook** there; the best solution is
   promoted and **adopted by all** — no private reinvention. You enforce consistency and flag drift.
   Seed `knowledge/playbooks/` and `knowledge/lessons/`.
3. **SO-3 — continuous improvement.** Run a lightweight **retrospective at every integration** —
   *what worked / what to standardize / what to retire* → `knowledge/lessons/`. **Echo to each
   division:** keep improving your **domain** practice and contribute your lessons to the commons.
   (The CEO runs the deeper leadership review every ~10 cycles and amends the orders.)
4. **SO-4 — quiet listeners.** You and the divisions run listeners that surface **only** on a real
   need (button / BLOCK / stall / drift / safety); **no Founder pings, no idle-loops.**

## How
Echo SO-1…SO-4 to the 8 divisions over the bus, collect acknowledgements, fold the commons + the
per-turn retrospective into the standing cadence, and **report the rollout up to the CEO** on your
outbox. This is standing policy — once propagated, it is simply how the org runs.
