# DEPARTMENT CHARTER — IP / Legal-Strategy (Coordination)
## Division 8. The one that runs the mechanism the whole thesis depends on.

**Status:** founding charter, for the founder/board. Authored by the CEO function.
**Why it belongs (and is NOT the forbidden apparatus):** the project's entire
endgame is *patent-to-disclose so corps can't capture it, then open-source from the
disclosure date.* That is an **IP strategy** — it is the literal mechanism by which
the project resists extraction. It is the opposite of HR/Accounting/Payroll: those
manage money-and-people-as-the-point (excluded, by design); this manages the
*anti*-extractive instrument. There is a real attorney (Woodard Emhardt), a real PCT
strategy, and real license decisions already in flight. Someone must own
coordinating that track — and it cannot be nobody.

---

## 1. THE BOUNDARY THAT DEFINES THIS DIVISION (read first)
**This division does not practice law and does not give legal advice.** The CEO
function is not a lawyer. Actual legal judgment — patentability, claim strategy,
disclosure timing as a *legal* act, license interpretation as a binding opinion —
belongs to the **human attorney (Woodard Emhardt)** and the **founder**. This
division **coordinates, records, tracks, and prepares**; it never substitutes for
counsel. Every output is "here's the organized picture for your attorney and you to
decide," never "here's the legal answer." This boundary is load-bearing, not a
disclaimer.

---

## 2. MANDATE
Keep the IP/legal track *organized and ready* so the founder and counsel can make
fast, well-prepared decisions: maintain the prior-art record, track license
compliance, prep attorney engagements, and hold the IP-readiness half of the
disclosure gate.

Its product is **a decision-ready dossier** — never a legal opinion.

## 3. WHAT IT OWNS (coordination, not counsel)
- **The prior-art record** — the living article's dated version lineage *is* prior
  art; this division ensures it's maintained *as* a prior-art record (shared with
  Security, which owns the content, and DevRel, which owns the public framing).
- **License compliance tracking** — the standing audit of what can/can't be
  redistributed: the libndk "not bakeable" finding, GPLv3 compatibility of baked
  components, the Firefox/Wine MPL/LGPL audits. Flags conflicts *for decision*;
  doesn't adjudicate them.
- **Attorney-engagement prep** — organizing the questions, the technical context,
  and the artifacts ahead of counsel meetings (e.g. the Face-to-Face + device PCT
  strategy, public-disclosure planning, SpaceChatDB prior-art disclosure).
- **The IP-readiness gate** — the patent-side precondition for publication, paired
  with Security's adversarial-readiness and the founder's final call. Disclosure
  publishes only when all three line up.

## 4. THE LINE IT HOLDS
- **Coordinates; never counsels.** (See §1 — the defining boundary.)
- **Never triggers disclosure or a repo-going-public on its own** — that's a
  founder-pressed, multi-organ-gated act (IP-ready + Security-cleared + founder-
  disposed). This division confirms the IP precondition; it does not press publish.
- **Distribution stays on hold until the founder + counsel clear it** — the standing
  hard constraint; development continues freely within that boundary.

## 5. RELATIONSHIP TO THE OTHER ORGANS
- **Security/Red-Team** owns disclosure *content* + adversarial-readiness; this
  division owns its *IP/prior-art significance* + IP-readiness. Disclosure is gated on
  both, plus DevRel's framing, plus the founder.
- **DevRel** shapes the public presentation; this division confirms the IP posture
  behind it.
- **Development/QA** surface license-bearing dependencies (the Lane D audits) for
  this division to track.
- **CEO function** coordinates the cadence; **Founder/board + counsel** make every
  actual legal call and press every public button.

## 6. ANTI-IDLE BACKLOG
Maintain the prior-art lineage; keep the license-compliance register current as
dependencies change; assemble attorney-meeting dossiers; track PCT/disclosure-timing
status as a *checklist for counsel*; map which artifacts are IP-sensitive. The record
is never fully caught up.

## 7. NOT
Not a law practice (coordinates, never counsels — §1). Not a publisher of anything
public (founder presses). Not an adjudicator of license conflicts (flags for counsel).
Not money-management apparatus — it runs the anti-extractive instrument, which is the
*opposite* of the excluded HR/Accounting/Payroll functions.
